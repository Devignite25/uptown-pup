our story

Dogs deserve beautiful things too

UpTown Pup started with a simple idea: dogs deserve accessories as special as they are. Each collar is hand-strung bead by bead from soft silicone beads — lightweight, wipeable, and made to turn heads at the dog park.

From everyday neutrals to Halloween pumpkins and Christmas candy canes, there's a design for every personality and every season. And because each one is made by hand, no two are exactly alike.
